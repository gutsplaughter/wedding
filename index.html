Venue

The Kempton New Church

Join us for our wedding ceremony at the Kempton New Church. This location is a central point in the community and reflects the values of charity and community that we hope to build in our own home. After the ceremony, we look forward to celebrating the start of our journey with all of you!

We want our ceremony to be a truly sacred and present moment for everyone. We kindly ask that you turn off all cell phones and cameras, and that the church be kept quiet during the service and special music. Please sit back, relax, and be fully present with us as we take this holy step together.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I bring my children?

While we are unable to accommodate additional guests beyond those named on the invitation, we warmly welcome infants-in-arms.

Can I bring a plus-one?

Your RSVP card indicates the number of seats reserved in your honor. We kindly ask that only those included in your reservation attend.

What time should I arrive?

Guests are welcome to arrive during the musical prelude before the ceremony begins. We recommend arriving a little early to enjoy the music and be seated comfortably. Ushers will be available to greet guests and assist with seating.

Is parking available?

Yes, parking is available at the church.

Will food be served?

Yes. The reception will include hors d'oeuvres during cocktail hour, followed by dinner and dessert.

What if it rains?

The reception will be held under a tent, with plans in place for inclement weather.

Can I take photos during the ceremony?

We kindly ask guests to silence their phones and refrain from taking photos during the ceremony so everyone can be fully present. There will be a photographer professionally capturing the event from the rear of the church.
